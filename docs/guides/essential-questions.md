# Ogeemo Essential Questions Catalog

This catalog defines core question coverage targets for guide generation and ingestion.

## Onboarding and Authentication
- How do I create my first account in Ogeemo?
- How do I sign in if I forgot my password?
- How do I switch from Google sign-in to email sign-in?
- How do I invite team members and assign roles?
- How do I verify my account has the right permissions?

## Contacts and CRM
- How do I create a new client contact?
- How do I convert a lead into an active client?
- How do I update contact details without losing history?
- How do I find duplicate contacts and merge them?
- How do I track a lead from unscheduled to completed?

## File and Evidence Management
- How do I upload files to the correct document folder?
- How do I link uploaded files to a contact or project?
- How do I recover a missing or misplaced document?
- How do I verify evidence is attached before submission?
- How do I enforce consistent folder naming for audits?

## Accounting and Finance
- How do I create and categorize a business expense?
- How do I post and review journal entries?
- How do I reconcile transactions and correct mismatches?
- How do I generate accounts receivable and payable summaries?
- How do I prepare records for month-end close?

## Payroll and HR
- How do I create an employee versus contractor record?
- How do I configure pay type and pay rate correctly?
- How do I run payroll and verify totals before finalization?
- How do I fix payroll errors after processing?
- How do I document contract-on-file and compliance flags?

## Reporting and Export
- How do I run analytics and reporting workflows?
- How do I export reports for audit or stakeholder review?
- How do I validate export completeness and format?
- How do I troubleshoot failed export actions?
- How do I re-run and compare reports across periods?

## Settings, Admin, and Compliance
- How do I configure role-based permissions safely?
- How do I update organization settings without breaking workflows?
- How do I review security rules impacts on app behavior?
- How do I audit who changed critical settings?
- How do I prepare compliance evidence for external review?

## AI Assistant and Troubleshooting
- Why did Ogeemo Assistant respond with missing context?
- How do I add a new guide so Assistant can answer a gap question?
- How do I re-ingest guides after app workflow changes?
- How do I verify vector ingestion completed successfully?
- How do I diagnose and fix assistant 500 errors?

## Coverage Notes
- Treat each question above as a workflow intent that should map to one or more guide documents.
- Mark each item in the coverage matrix as Missing, Draft, Verified, or Published.
- Prioritize critical operational and compliance questions first.
