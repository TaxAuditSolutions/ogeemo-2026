## 1. Executive Summary

**Ogeemo** is a comprehensive, cloud-based business management platform (ERP) built to unify the entire operational backbone of small businesses, professional service firms, and independent consultants. Rather than forcing users to stitch together a dozen disconnected SaaS subscriptions, Ogeemo consolidates accounting, human resources, customer relationship management (CRM), project management, document management, and AI-powered automation into a single, deeply integrated ecosystem.

The platform was built by **Tax Audit Solutions (TAS)**, a real-world accounting and compliance firm, as an internal tool to solve their own operational pain points. After proving its value in the trenches of real audit work, TAS is now bringing Ogeemo to market as a commercial product. The company is headquartered in **Vancouver, BC, Canada**.

Ogeemo is currently in a **go-to-market ready state** — the core platform is built, operational, and ready for commercial scale.

---

## 2. What Ogeemo Is

Ogeemo is best understood as a **"digital nervous system"** for small and mid-sized businesses. It is not a single-purpose tool; it is a unified platform where every module is natively connected to every other module. A time entry flows directly into payroll. A client contact is linked to their invoices, projects, and documents. A calendar event generates a billable time log. This is what Ogeemo calls the **"Spider Web" architecture** — a single interconnected data graph that eliminates the "administrative gaps" that plague traditional software setups.

### Technical Foundation
- **Frontend:** Next.js 15 (React 19) — modern, fast, SEO-friendly
- **Backend:** Google Firebase (Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Storage, Authentication)
- **AI Layer:** Google Genkit + Google AI — powers natural-language commands and intelligent automation
- **Hosting:** Cloud-native, scalable infrastructure
- **Security:** Enterprise-grade Firestore security rules, role-based access control

## 3. What Ogeemo Does — Core Modules

### 3.1 Accounting & Financials ("BKS" — Bookkeeping Kept Simple)
The financial engine is the heart of Ogeemo. It is a **cash-basis accounting system** designed around one principle: **audit-readiness as a daily discipline, not a year-end scramble.**

- **General Ledger** — Track income, expenses, and account balances with full journal entry support
- **Invoicing & Billing** — Generate professional invoices with custom line items and payment tracking
- **Quotes & Estimates** — Create, send, and convert quotes into invoices
- **Payroll System** — Manage employees, run payroll, calculate deductions, and handle remittances
- **Accounts Receivable & Payable** — Full AR/AP cycle management
- **Bank Statement Reconciliation** — Match transactions to ledger entries
- **Capital Asset Management** — Track depreciable assets (CCA)
- **Loan Manager** — Track loan balances and amortization
- **Sales Tax Calculator** — Prepare for quarterly tax remittances
- **Accrual Accounting** — Full accrual adjustments support
- **Financial Reports** — Income statements, financial snapshots, accrual adjustment reports
- **Petty Cash Management**
- **Work Orders** — Track operational work and link it to billing

**Key Differentiator:** Every single ledger entry can be linked to a digital source document (receipt, invoice, contract), creating what Ogeemo calls a **"Black Box of Evidence"** — a defensible audit trail that accountants and tax authorities respect.

### 3.2 Human Resources & Workforce Management
- **Employee & Worker Profiles** — Manage roles, permissions, and worker data
- **Time Tracking** — Log hours with 5-minute granularity (the "Temporal Matrix"), capturing even "invisible work" like quick client calls
- **Leave Management** — Track time off and vacation requests
- **Field App** — A mobile-optimized interface for workers in the field
- **Payroll Integration** — Time logs flow directly into payroll calculations

### 3.3 CRM & Contact Management
- **Contact Directory** — Centralized database for clients, vendors, and associates
- **Lead Tracking** — Sales pipeline and inquiry management
- **CRM Action Tracking** — Track every interaction and touchpoint
- **Contact Folders** — Organize contacts systematically
- **Relationship Intelligence** — View communication history, project status, and financial balance in a single contact profile

### 3.4 Operations & Project Management
- **Project Forge** — Kanban-style project boards with reusable templates
- **Tasks & To-Dos** — Task tracking and assignment
- **Inventory Management** — Track stock levels, units of measure, and items
- **Calendar** — Centralized scheduling with 5-minute slot granularity
- **Meetings** — Agenda management and meeting notes
- **Document Manager** — Cloud storage with Google Drive integration
- **Email Hub** — Centralized email management

### 3.5 AI & Automation
- **AI Dispatch Terminal** — Natural-language command interface (e.g., "Start meeting with Acme Corp" triggers the timer and opens notes)
- **AI Image Generation** — Built-in image generation for marketing and content
- **AI Search** — Advanced search across all platform data
- **Sandbox Agent** — An AI assistant trained on Ogeemo's knowledge base to help users navigate the platform

### 3.6 Specialized Modules
- **Hytexercise** — A wellness and exercise management module
- **Master Mind / Command Centre** — A high-fidelity temporal execution engine for scheduling and live work sessions
- **Idea Board** — Brainstorming and idea capture
- **Marketing Manager** — Marketing campaign and content tools
- **Reports Hub** — Work activity summaries, client statements, time log reports, and advanced search
- **Backups & Data Portability** — Secure backup and export capabilities

### 3.7 The "Action Chip" System
Ogeemo's signature navigation feature. Users can customize their own dashboard of "Action Chips" — quick-access buttons that carry context across the platform. This eliminates menu-hunting and allows each user to sculpt Ogeemo to their specific workflow.

## 5. Business Model & Pricing

### Current Pricing Structure
- **Circle Membership:** $25.00/month (total)
- **Includes:** 5 users
- **Additional users:** $5.00 each
- **Free trial:** 30 days
- **Price lock:** Membership fee is locked for life — no surprise increases

### Revenue Streams (Current & Planned)
1. **SaaS Subscriptions** — Primary recurring revenue
2. **Mentor Marketplace** (planned) — A platform where members can hire "Certified Mentors" (vetted CPAs, lawyers, bookkeepers) for 1-on-1 consulting. Ogeemo takes a **10–20% platform commission** on all transactions
3. **Community Ecosystem** — "The Ogeemo Web" acts as a free-to-enter community gateway that drives lead generation and software adoption

### Anti-Greed Value Proposition
Ogeemo's pricing philosophy is a market differentiator:
- **No feature gating** — Every member gets the full platform
- **No tier traps** — No "Pro" vs "Basic" upselling
- **Ethical exit** — Data is fully portable; no hostage situations
- **Community dividends** — Members vote on the product roadmap

## 6. Marketing Potential & Competitive Advantage

### 6.1 Market Opportunity
The global SMB software market is massive and fragmented. Small businesses typically use **7–10 different software tools** to run their operations (e.g., QuickBooks for accounting, HubSpot for CRM, Asana for projects, Gusto for payroll, Google Workspace for documents). Ogeemo replaces all of these with a single platform.

**Total Addressable Market (TAM):**
- 33+ million small businesses in North America alone
- 400+ million globally
- The SMB software spend averages $1,000–$5,000/year per business
- Even capturing 0.01% of the North American market = 3,300+ paying customers

### 6.2 Key Competitive Differentiators

| **AI-Native** | AI is built into the core (AI Dispatch, AI Search, AI Assistant), not bolted on as a feature |

| **Mentor Marketplace** | A two-sided marketplace that creates network effects and a secondary revenue stream |
| **No Feature Gating** | Eliminates the "upgrade anxiety" that drives customers away from competitors |

Ogeemo sits at the intersection of several software categories:
- **ERP** (replaces: NetSuite, SAP Business One — but at a fraction of the cost)
- **Accounting** (replaces: QuickBooks, Xero, FreshBooks)

### 6.4 Go-To-Market Strategy
The platform has a built-in GTM engine through its dual-ecosystem model:

1. **Phase 1 — The Gateway:** Free community signup (The Ogeemo Web) acts as a lead-generation funnel
2. **Phase 2 — Software Hooks:** Community members convert to paid software members via SSO
3. **Phase 3 — Mentor Marketplace:** Certified Mentors (CPAs, lawyers, bookkeepers) offer paid consulting through the platform, creating a services marketplace
4. **Phase 4 — Founders Tier:** The first 500 paid members receive a permanent "Founders" badge, creating FOMO-driven early adoption
